## Brand & Style

This design system establishes an authoritative, collegiate, and reassuringly modern visual language for an elite English language and competitive examination academy. The interface merges traditional academic pedigree with digital precision, projecting scholarship, mentorship, and tangible student success under institutional leadership.

### Target Audience & Emotional Intent
- **Demographic**: Ambitious aspirants, university students, career professionals, and discerning parents seeking mastery in spoken English, competitive examinations, and advanced grammar.
- **Emotional Cadence**: Inspiring yet disciplined; reassuringly authoritative; structured, intellectual, and warmly supportive. The environment feels like stepping into an endowed university library augmented with modern digital efficiency.

### Design Movement
- **Academic Modernism with Editorial Discipline**: Structured content blocks, crisp hairline borders, deep pigment-rich field washes, and prominent typographic contrast. The style eschews playful minimalism in favor of structured stature, deliberate whitespace, and scholarly clarity.

## Colors

The palette balances profound institutional authority with sophisticated neutral tones. Charcoal grey anchors the primary visual architecture, while muted slate and cool blue convey scholastic focus and guidance.

### Palette Architecture
- **Primary Charcoal (`#3A3B3C`)**: Anchor for top navigation, primary actions, dense institutional footers, and prominent serif headlines. Projects permanence, discipline, and scholarly weight.
- **Muted Slate (`#667A85`)**: Reserved for high-value calls to action, active batch enrollment states, distinction honors, key dates, and pedagogical highlights.
- **Collegiate Indigo (`#69769E`)**: Supporting interactive states, secondary buttons, student status indicators, and link accents.
- **Neutral Stone (`#74777A`)**: Structural body copy, metadata labels, and secondary interface icons.
- **Surface & Backgrounds**:
  - `surface-canvas`: `#F8FAFC` (Slate-tinted crisp white) ensures prolonged reading comfort without pure-white eye strain.
  - `surface-card`: `#FFFFFF` (Pure white) reserved for elevated interactive modules and structured cards.
  - `surface-muted`: `#F1F5F9` for data grids, batch schedule blocks, and code/grammar rule panels.
  - `border-subtle`: `#E2E8F0` provides hairline definition throughout data modules.

## Typography

The typographical pairing expresses academic gravitas juxtaposed with digital utility. `Playfair Display` evokes classical British and Ivy League publication traditions, establishing immediate pedagogical credibility. `Plus Jakarta Sans` delivers crisp readability for syllabi, scheduling data, lexical examples, and mobile-first student dashboards.

### Editorial Guidelines
- Use `Playfair Display` exclusively for page headers, course titles, testimonial pull quotes, and mentor credentials.
- Render all batch status tags, table headers, and form labels in uppercase or title-case `Plus Jakarta Sans` with tracked spacing (`0.02em` - `0.08em`) to sustain structured visual order.
- Maintain a comfortable reading length of 60–75 characters per line for long-form curriculum descriptions using `body-md` or `body-lg`.

## Layout & Spacing

The structural layout relies on an anchored 12-column grid system bounded by generous macro margins, maintaining an unhurried, editorial feel.

### Breakpoints & Reflow Behavior
- **Desktop (1280px and above)**: 12-column layout, max container width of 1240px, `3rem` canvas margins, and `1.5rem` gutters. Course modules and fee schedules run in asymmetrical 8/4 splits to emphasize core learning outcomes alongside registration panels.
- **Tablet (768px – 1024px)**: 8-column layout, `2rem` margins, and `1.25rem` gutters. Complex batch comparisons transition into tabbed or swipeable carousel modules.
- **Mobile (320px – 767px)**: 4-column layout, `1.25rem` margins, and `1rem` gutters. High-density information stacks linearly, with fixed bottom action strips for instant batch counseling or direct admissions inquiries.

### Spacing Rhythms
Component interiors deploy an 8px modular baseline (`space-xs` through `space-xl`). Academic cards prioritize distinct vertical spacing between module titles, schedule badges, and enrollment triggers.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy emphasizes tactile materiality rather than exaggerated floating effects. The elevation mimics stacked fine-laid bond paper, architectural desk blotters, and bound dossiers.

### Hierarchy Model
- **Level 0 (Flat Ground)**: `surface-canvas` (`#F8FAFC`). Flat background with zero shadow.
- **Level 1 (Resting Cards & Course Modules)**: Pure white surface with a crisp hairline border (`1px solid #E2E8F0`) and an ambient, low-spread shadow: `0 1px 3px rgba(58, 59, 60, 0.05), 0 10px 15px -3px rgba(58, 59, 60, 0.04)`.
- **Level 2 (Hovered Offerings & Batch Tiles)**: Raised card state accented with a subtle neutral rim: `0 10px 25px -5px rgba(58, 59, 60, 0.08), 0 8px 10px -6px rgba(58, 59, 60, 0.03)` with border shifting to `rgba(102, 122, 133, 0.4)`.
- **Level 3 (Modals, Admissions Drawers, & Key Notifications)**: Deep focus shadow tinted with charcoal pigment: `0 20px 25px -5px rgba(58, 59, 60, 0.15), 0 8px 10px -6px rgba(58, 59, 60, 0.1)`.
- **Accent Elevation (Mentor & Institutional Spotlights)**: Top edge 3px accent stroke in primary slate (`#667A85`), giving the component an anchored, official crest presence.

## Shapes

The design system employs a soft, restrained curvature (`roundedness: 1`), projecting stability, institutional tradition, and disciplined precision.

### Application Specs
- **Cards, Modules, & Data Tables**: `0.25rem` (4px) to `0.5rem` (8px). Corners feel structured and crisp, avoiding playful or toy-like softness.
- **Form Controls & Inputs**: Standardized at `0.25rem` (4px) radius, preserving the aesthetic of formal legal or collegiate application records.
- **Buttons**: `0.25rem` (4px) corner radius for primary and secondary actions.
- **Status Badges & Category Chips**: Strict pill contours (`9999px`) used selectively to contrast against rectangular cards and immediately demarcate batch timing or admission status.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (Admissions / Enroll)**: Charcoal background (`#3A3B3C`), crisp white text, 4px radius, padded with `0.75rem 1.5rem`. Subtle hover transition to collegiate indigo (`#69769E`) with an active scale state of `0.99`.
- **Secondary Action (Syllabus Download / Batch Details)**: Transparent background, `1.5px solid #3A3B3C`, text in `#3A3B3C`. Hover state fills with `rgba(58, 59, 60, 0.04)`.
- **Accent Action (Urgent Seat Allotment / Scholarships)**: Muted slate background (`#667A85`), text in `#FFFFFF`, with hover shift to `#4E5D66`.

### Batch Status & Academic Badges
- **Admissions Open**: Background `rgba(102, 122, 133, 0.12)`, text `#4E5D66`, border `1px solid rgba(102, 122, 133, 0.3)`. Text rendered in `label-badge` uppercase with a leading pulse indicator dot.
- **Batch Commenced / Closed**: Slate fill (`#F1F5F9`), text `#64748B`, border `1px solid #CBD5E1`.
- **Weekend / Fast-Track**: Charcoal subtle fill (`rgba(58, 59, 60, 0.08)`), text `#3A3B3C`, border `1px solid rgba(58, 59, 60, 0.15)`.

### Course Cards
- **Base Architecture**: Pure white background, `1px solid #E2E8F0` border, `0.5rem` roundedness. Interior padding structured at `1.5rem`.
- **Header Slot**: Playfair Display course title over a dual-column metadata row (Batch Timing, Total Weeks, Target Exams).
- **Mentor Endorsement Ribbon**: Discrete bottom divider carrying a warm sign-off: "Curated & Led by Hirdesh Sir", styled with `body-sm` italic slate text alongside a monogram seal.

### Form Inputs & Selectors
- **Fields**: Background `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #CBD5E1`, text `#3A3B3C`. Focus state eliminates default browser rings, applying a crisp `#3A3B3C` outline with a delicate `rgba(58, 59, 60, 0.08)` outer glow.
- **Labels**: Rendered in `title-md` or `label-lg`, resting strictly above the input for unambiguous clarity.

### Testimonial & Result Showcase Cards
- **Format**: Off-white textured surface, charcoal quotation marks in display size, student name and qualifying rank/exam in `label-lg`, accompanied by a subtle slate verification checkmark badge.
